Strum Trainer

Set a strumming pattern, pick a tempo, and follow big arrows in full screen.

1 · Your pattern

Each symbol is one half-beat (an eighth note). Use D, U, X or C for a chuck (muted, percussive strum: lay your palm across the strings as you strike), and - for a silent slot. Click a box above to cycle it. Numbers under boxes are the count: 1 & 2 & 3 & 4 &.

2 · Chords (optional)

loop(s) of the pattern

Leave empty to practise the strum only.

3 · Song lyrics (optional)

Put chords on their own line directly above the lyric line, lined up with the word where you change. [Verse] / [Chorus] lines become headings. Each chord in the song is one change, and the "Change chord every N loops" setting above sets how long each lasts.
